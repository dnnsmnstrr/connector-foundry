How it fits together

One slot convention. Every part speaks it.

Every part in the catalogue is modelled the same way, whichever system it comes from. That is what lets a part from one system attach to a part from any other — and what the app's three tabs are built on.

  1. 1

    Functional face down

    The side that does the work — Gridfinity feet, GoPro prongs, a Skådis hook — is the part's bottom, which is also the way it prints.

  2. 2

    A mount slot on top

    Its top carries a named slot. Bigger parts add a grid of them, and parts with more faces add slots on their sides.

  3. 3

    Anything attaches

    Put one part's slot on another's and they mate: fused into one body, or held by a real joint you print and then bolt, snap or pin together.

Library1

Pick a part. Drag it to size.

Every part is parametric. Set grid units, heights and hole options in the sidebar, or grab a square handle in the 3D view and drag it, as in TinkerCAD. Sizes snap to each system's own steps — a Gridfinity unit is 42 mm — and the part renders once, when you let go.

  • Click a handle to type an exact size
  • Keep your favourite values as your own defaults
  • Download the STL, or paste it straight into Layerling
Print your first part

Bench2

Combine parts from different systems.

Start from any base part and click one of its green slot markers to attach another. Choose how the two join, then turn, shift or move the part until it sits right. Every printable body downloads as an STL of its own.

  • Up to three parts on a base, stacked on each other's slots
  • Start from a model of your own: STL, STEP or 3MF
  • Share a bench as a link, or keep it as a config file
More about the Bench

Holes3

Holes exactly where they belong.

Click a face and the hole snaps to its center, its quarters, its corners or a guide line between them. Pick a screw or a connector slot from the presets, and the part — or a model of your own — comes back drilled.

  • Socket caps, countersinks, heat-set inserts, nut traps
  • Slots for openConnect, MultiConnect, Skådis and MOLLE
  • Custom Gridfinity inserts, cut from a filled bin
